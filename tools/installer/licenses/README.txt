What the Cybertron Escalation installer puts on your computer besides the demo:

- python\: Python (https://www.python.org), with its own licence in
  python\LICENSE.txt, and the numpy, Pillow and fontTools packages (their
  licences in python\Lib\site-packages\<package>.dist-info). It runs the asset
  build's scripts.
- tools\umodel\: UE Viewer, under the MIT licence in UEViewer.txt.
- tools\ffmpeg\ffmpeg.exe: ffmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org, GPL version 3), which the
  game-files builder downloads from https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/ the first
  time it runs; it isn't part of the installer.

The game files in assets\ are built on your computer from your own copy of
Transformers: Fall of Cybertron; they are its publisher's, for your own use.
